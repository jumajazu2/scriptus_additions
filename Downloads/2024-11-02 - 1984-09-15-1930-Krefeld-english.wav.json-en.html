We are united in your divine love.

O Lord, we long to see your word confirmed.

Lord, may it come to life.

Faithful Lord, grant us the grace before your face to see every promise fulfilled.

And if it is in your will, may we make your offer of grace to this generation.

Lord, how grateful our hearts are, but perhaps not grateful enough for what you have granted us.

What a grace and what a privilege, in the midst of all interpretations, in the midst of all confusion, to become silent in your presence and to hear your holy word as spoken by your holy mouth.

O let us tremble, tremble in your presence.

Lord, you see our inability, but we believe in your omnipotence.

Come to our aid, give us an upturn, O Lord, a turnaround, a new encounter with you.

We thank you.

You cried out, "It is finished."

And on that day you will proclaim, "It is done." Hallelujah.

Death will be no more.

Hallelujah.

You will wipe away all tears and dwell among your own.

O Lord, we thank you for this, because we believe that your word is true and that we have part in what you are doing.

We worship you.

Praise be to your holy name.

Amen.
